Matchup Simulator

Pick a season and a team on each side — any real NBA team-season from 1996-97 through 2025-26 — and simulate a real best-of-7 series against the actual DBA 2 possession engine, running live on this machine — nothing here is precomputed.

VS
Rosters are real: each team-season's top 10 players by real minutes played that season, derived straight from seeds/real_players/<season>.json. Team identity reflects each franchise's current name/city even for old seasons (e.g. pre-2008 Thunder entries are really the Seattle SuperSonics) — a limit of the underlying data, not something invented here.
Reading these: position labels are a coarse guess (the underlying data only distinguishes G / F / C, not PG-vs-SG or SF-vs-PF), and playing time reflects each player's rated overall rather than their real historical role — a bench player can land starter minutes here if his rated overall is high enough. Team identity and player ratings are real; rotations, game flow, and the series outcome are the sim engine's own, freshly computed on this run.
